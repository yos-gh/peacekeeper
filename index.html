PEACEKEEPER

KEEP THE BALANCE
Source

Two autonomous factions are locked in an endless territorial war. Intervene with a neutral ball, protect the space between them, and keep the Doomsday Clock from reaching midnight.

Controls

  • Move: A / D or left/right arrows
  • Gamepad: D-pad or left stick
  • Mobile: virtual analog stick
  • Return to title: Esc
  • Fullscreen: upper-right button

Objective

Aim at the stronger faction. Your ball restores cells to neutral territory, but every miss advances the clock.

Doomsday Clock

The clock stops at exact parity and accelerates quadratically as the territorial gap grows. At 24:00:00, the world ends.

Inspiration

Inspired by Pong Wars by Koen van Gilst. Built with Godot 4.
