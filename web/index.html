PEACEKEEPER is a minimalist arcade game. Two autonomous factions fight an endless territorial war, and you defend the neutral ground between them with a paddle and a ball.

How to play

Goal

Aim at the stronger faction. Your ball turns the cells it touches back into neutral territory.

Doomsday Clock

Every miss advances the clock, and a growing territorial gap speeds it up. The world ends at 24:00:00.
